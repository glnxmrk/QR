About SJISecure

SJISecure is an advanced school attendance monitoring system designed to provide real-time updates to parents and secure tracking for the school administration.

Built with privacy and efficiency in mind, it uses QR Code technology to quickly log student entry and exit times, automatically composing SMS notifications for parents.

Installation Guide

iOS (iPhone/iPad)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Menu (three dots).
  3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Follow the prompts to install.

PC / Mac

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for the Install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click it and select Install.
